Welcome to **PingKarthik**! Instead of letting project notes, random fixes, and half-finished experiments get lost in local text files or buried in chat logs, I finally put together a dedicated place to document everything.

### What I'll Be Posting Here

This isn't going to be a formal tutorial site—more of a running log of whatever I'm currently building, breaking, or experimenting with:

- **Hardware & Microcontroller Builds:** ESP32 and Arduino projects, sensor setups, NFC experiments, and hardware workarounds.
- **OS & System Deep-Dives:** Arch Linux setups, Windows LTSC tweaks, VMware lab configurations, and bootloader troubleshooting.
- **Code & Automation:** Python scripts, web tools, and small utilities I build from scratch.
- **Random Teardowns & Repairs:** Breathing new life into older laptops and figuring out why something stopped working.

### How This Site Works

Everything here is built cleanly with **HTML**, **CSS**, and **Markdown** hosted directly on GitHub Pages—no heavy frameworks, no trackers, and fast load times.

More posts and build logs coming soon. Stay tuned!
